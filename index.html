🎮 How to play

  • Move: arrow keys or W A S D
  • Use / pick up / continue: press E (or Enter)
  • Menus: arrow keys to choose, E to press
  • Jump: Space, only outside the base bubble
  • Pause menu: press Esc
  • 🚙 Rover: stand by it and press Q to drive (E = fast travel, Q = get out)
  • ⛰️ Walk to the top or bottom edge of the map to reach the poles
  • 🚙 Goal: launch research to Earth, build the rover, find Sam!
  • 🧹 Clean the solar panels before bed AND after waking up
  • 🖥️ Computer: the only way to talk to Earth
  • ❄️🪨 Explore! Ice and rocks are far from base
  • 💨 Air runs down outside. Go back in the bubble
  • 🛏️ Sleep in the evening, or you will faint
  • 🔧 Machines take time to switch. Plan ahead!
